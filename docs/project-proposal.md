# Project Proposal

**Project Title**

**Independent Game Development as a Catalyst for the Evolution of the Video Game Industry**

## 1. Background and Problem Statement

Independent game development has become an important part of the video game industry. Independent developers can explore alternative game mechanics, artistic styles, narrative approaches, and production methods that may differ from those used by large studios.

However, independent development also presents challenges, including limited financial resources, small team sizes, marketing difficulties, and competition in digital distribution platforms.

Understanding how independent game development contributes to changes in the video game industry requires examining both its creative opportunities and its economic and technical limitations.

This project will investigate the role of independent game development in the evolution of the video game industry through a review of scientific literature and a comparative analysis of selected independent games. The research findings will inform the design and planned development of an original 2D narrative game, *One More Day*.

## 2. Project Aim

The aim of this project is to investigate how independent game development contributes to innovation and change in the video game industry and to apply selected findings to the design and development of an original independent game.

## 3. Research Objectives

1. Define independent game development and identify its main characteristics.

1. Examine the historical, technological, economic, and creative factors that have influenced independent game development.

1. Investigate how independent games contribute to innovation in game design, interactive storytelling, and production practices.

1. Identify the main opportunities and limitations faced by independent game developers.

1. Compare selected independent games using a consistent set of analytical criteria.

1. Evaluate alternative technologies and design approaches relevant to the practical project.

1. Apply selected findings to the design and planned implementation of *One More Day*.

1. Develop a plan for evaluating the resulting prototype and documenting its limitations.

## 4. Research Questions

**RQ1:** What technological, economic, and cultural factors have contributed to the development of independent game development?

**RQ2:** In what ways have independent games contributed to innovation in game design, interactive storytelling, and game production?

**RQ3:** What opportunities and limitations characterize independent game development compared with larger-scale game production?

**RQ4:** Which design and development approaches identified through the research can be applied to the practical project, *One More Day*?

## 5. Target Audience

The research is intended for readers interested in independent game development, game design, video game industry studies, and the development of small-scale games.

The practical project is intended for players interested in narrative-driven 2D games. The specific target audience and relevant player needs will be refined through preliminary research and discussions with potential users.

## 6. Project Scope

**The research will focus on independent game development, including:**

- The definition and characteristics of independent game development.

- Technological accessibility and digital distribution.

- Creative experimentation and innovation in game design.

- Interactive storytelling and artistic expression.

- Economic, organizational, and production constraints.

- The relationship between independent developers and the wider video game industry.

The practical component will involve the design and planned development of *One More Day*, an original 2D narrative game.

The practical work will focus on defining the game's concept, core gameplay and narrative requirements, architecture, technology choices, prototype, and evaluation approach.

The project will not attempt to establish a universal causal relationship between independent game development and the evolution of the entire video game industry. Instead, it will examine relevant evidence from existing research and selected case studies, then apply specific findings to the practical project.

## 7. Research Methodology

**The project will use the following methods:**

**Literature review:** Analysis of scientific publications and other relevant sources concerning independent game development, industry evolution, game design, and production practices.

**Comparative case study:** Examination of selected independent games using consistent criteria, including narrative design, gameplay innovation, production context, and relevant technical or commercial characteristics.

**Technology comparison:** Evaluation of alternative technologies and development approaches against the requirements of the practical project.

**User research:** Preliminary discussions with potential players to identify relevant expectations and usability considerations for the planned game.

**Prototype development and evaluation:** Design and implementation of a prototype, followed by functional testing and an appropriate evaluation of the implemented features.

The selection of sources, case studies, participants, and evaluation criteria will be documented and justified as the project progresses.

## 8. Expected Outcomes

**The expected outcomes are:**

1. A structured review of relevant scientific literature.

1. An analysis of the role of independent game development in the evolution of the video game industry.

1. A comparative study of selected independent games.

1. A documented comparison of alternative technologies and design approaches.

1. A set of requirements and architectural diagrams for *One More Day*.

1. A prototype demonstrating selected aspects of the proposed game.

1. An evaluation plan and documented findings from the available implementation.

1. A discussion of the project's limitations and potential future development.

These are intended outcomes; they are not presented as completed results.

## 9. Project Management

Project development will be documented in a Git repository. Research materials, design documents, diagrams, implementation work, and tests will be maintained in an organized project structure.

A YouTrack project will be used to track tasks, priorities, responsibilities, acceptance criteria, deadlines, and project risks.

Progress will be documented through meaningful commits and updates to the project documentation.

## 10. Limitations

The research will be limited by the availability and scope of relevant publications, the selection of case studies, and the time available for the project.

Findings from selected games will not necessarily generalize to the entire independent game industry. User research will provide preliminary insights rather than statistically representative conclusions.

The scope of the practical implementation will depend on the available development time and the requirements established during the design stage.

## 11. Expected Deliverables

- Project proposal and research documentation.

- Literature review and publication analysis table.

- Comparative analysis of selected independent games.

- Technology comparison.

- Functional and non-functional requirements.

- System context, use case, and component diagrams.

- UX materials and prototype.

- Verification and evaluation plan.

- Git repository and YouTrack project.

- Final documentation of the implemented work and its limitations.
