# Documentation Index

This directory contains the research, design, user-research, architecture, and evaluation documents for **One More Day** and its diploma topic, *Independent Game Development as a Catalyst for the Evolution of the Video Game Industry*.

## Document Formats

- **Markdown (`.md`)** is the version-controlled source of truth. It is best for browsing on GitHub, reviewing changes, and tracking edits.
- **Microsoft Word (`.docx`)** copies are provided for academic review and submission in the [Word documents folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ctH97rbreNvqTOoONxiDKU9G49EQ-rkG?usp=sharing).

Word copies are not automatically synchronised with Markdown. When a document changes, update the Markdown source and regenerate or revise the Word copy before using it for submission.

## Research Status and Limitations

The user-research report documents **six exploratory text-based interviews**: three primarily from player perspectives and three from development or industry perspectives. The sample is small, non-random, and non-representative; the interview guides varied, and the participants were not shown a specific game concept or playable prototype. The findings therefore inform preliminary research and project planning, but do not validate the specific narrative choice or demonstrate usability.

Technology comparisons, design assumptions, and evaluation criteria remain provisional until tested. The evaluation plan describes proposed procedures and test cases; it does not report completed technical tests or player-evaluation results.

The project is being developed individually. Time available for parallel implementation, testing, and refinement is therefore limited, making scope control and staged delivery important.
