
# One More Day

**Independent Game Development as a Catalyst for the Evolution of the Video Game Industry**

## Overview

This repository contains the research and development materials for a diploma project investigating the role of independent game development in the evolution of the video game industry.

The practical component of the project is **One More Day**, a planned 2D top-down narrative indie game focused on personal relationships, serious illness, emotional experiences, and player choice.

The project explores how independent game development can provide opportunities for distinctive narrative approaches, experimental design, and emotionally driven player experiences.

## Research Objectives

The research aims to:

- Examine the role of independent game development in the evolution of the video game industry.
- Analyze selected independent games and their contributions to game design, narrative, and player experience.
- Compare existing narrative games using consistent evaluation criteria.
- Evaluate potential technologies and tools for developing a small-scale narrative indie game.
- Apply the research findings to the design and development of *One More Day*.

## About the Game

*One More Day* is a narrative-driven game following April, an art university student whose life changes after she receives a cancer diagnosis.

The story is structured into three chapters:

1. **Life Before Diagnosis** — introduces April, her everyday life, friendships, family, and studies.
2. **Diagnosis and Hospitalization** — explores the emotional consequences of the diagnosis and its impact on her relationships.
3. **The Final Choice** — presents two different ways of responding to the circumstances: maintaining connections with others or withdrawing from social life.

The player's final choice leads to different scenes, interactions, and emotional experiences. Neither path is designed as a guaranteed happy ending, and the choice is not intended to serve as a simple moral judgment of the protagonist.

The game aims to explore how narrative structure and player agency can shape emotional engagement.

## Technology

The following technologies are under consideration for the project:

- **Unity 6** — game engine.
- **C#** — gameplay and interaction programming.
- **Yarn Spinner** — dialogue and narrative branching.
- **FL Studio** — original music production.

These choices remain subject to technical comparison and validation during the research and prototyping stages.

## Research and Evaluation

The research combines literature analysis, comparative case studies of independent games, comparison of existing narrative games, and evaluation of potential development technologies.

The practical component will be evaluated through a prototype and a verification plan addressing narrative progression, dialogue branching, player interaction, and audio integration.

Evaluation criteria and measurement procedures will be documented as they are finalized.

## Scope and Limitations

The project focuses on the relationship between independent game development and selected forms of innovation in the video game industry. It does not assume that all independent games are innovative or that independent development alone causes industry-wide change.

The practical component is a small-scale narrative game intended to demonstrate selected design ideas rather than reproduce the scope of a commercial production.

## Academic Context

This repository supports a diploma project on independent game development and its role in the evolution of the video game industry.
